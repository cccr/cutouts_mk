Cutouts is a short experimental stop-motion film created through a handmade process, combining cutout animation, photography, and physical objects.

After completing the film, I realized that its visual language could hold more than one musical interpretation. I invited musicians and composers from around the world to create their own scores for the film.

The result is 10 different versions of Cutouts. The film remains the same, while each score brings its own musical language, rhythm, and atmosphere.
